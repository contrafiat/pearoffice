The first release of PearOffice: spreadsheets, documents and slides you share and edit together, straight between your devices. No accounts, no servers, no cloud.

## New Features

- **Spreadsheets** with around 400 formulas, charts, filters, comments, cell formats and colour rules
- **Documents** with headings, lists, tables, pictures and links
- **Slides** with layouts, shapes, pictures, charts and four themes, and a presenter view for a second screen
- **Edit together, live.** See who else has a file open and where they are working
- **Share with a link.** Each invite works once and lasts 24 hours. Invite people to edit or just to view
- **Open and save Office files.** Excel, Word and PowerPoint files, plus CSV, Markdown and PDF
- **Use all your devices.** Link your phone and computers once and they keep the same files
- **Recovery phrase.** 12 words bring your identity, and your files if you use a seeder, back on a new device
- **Optional seeder.** A device you leave running keeps your files reachable when everyone else is offline, and it cannot read them. Run it from the desktop app, on a Linux server or on Umbrel or StartOS

## Downloads

- **Linux:** the AppImage, or the .deb for Debian and Ubuntu
- **Mac:** the .dmg for Apple silicon (arm64) or Intel (x64)
- **Windows:** PearOffice-Setup. Windows may warn that the app is from an unknown publisher; choose More info, then Run anyway
- **Seeder for Linux servers:** pearoffice-seeder for x64 or arm64

The desktop app tells you when a new version is out.
