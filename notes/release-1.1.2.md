PearOffice 1.1.2 adds templates and links in spreadsheets.

## New Features

- Templates. Mark any sheet, document or deck as a template from its menu. New then offers it next to a blank file, and picking it gives you a fresh copy of your own. Keep a template to yourself or share it with people the usual way. Comments stay behind in the template
- Ready-made templates. New comes with a Budget sheet, a Recipe, Meeting notes and a Simple deck to start from
- Links in spreadsheet cells. Add a link to a web page, an email address, another tab, a cell, a block of cells or a named range. Use the Link button, Ctrl+K or right-click a cell. Ctrl+click (Cmd+click on a Mac) a linked cell to follow it
- Web addresses typed into a cell become links on their own
- The HYPERLINK formula works, as in Excel
- Links come along when you open or save .xlsx and .ods files. Links to web pages stay clickable in PDFs

## Improvements

- Right-click a file, or press and hold it on a phone, to open its menu
- A long list of templates gets a search box and buttons to show only sheets, documents or slides

## Downloads

- Linux: the AppImage, or the .deb for Debian and Ubuntu
- Mac: the .dmg for Apple silicon (arm64) or Intel (x64)
- Windows: PearOffice-Setup. Windows may warn that the app is from an unknown publisher; choose More info, then Run anyway

The desktop app tells you when a new version is out.
