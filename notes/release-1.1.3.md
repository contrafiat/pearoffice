PearOffice 1.1.3 brings smart chips, coloured dropdowns and links between your files.

## New Features

- Coloured dropdowns. A dropdown list in a sheet can show each choice as a coloured pill, as in Google Sheets. Choose Dropdown from a cell's menu and the values already in those cells become the choices, each with its own colour. Tap a choice in the setup to change its colour
- More than one choice. Turn on "Allow more than one choice" and tick as many items as you like. Each one shows as its own pill
- Smart chips. Type @ in a cell to add a date, a person from the file or one of your files or folders. People show with their picture and current name and files with their icon. Dates get a calendar button for changing them
- Links to your files and folders. Link a cell to another PearOffice file or one of your folders from the Link button's Go to list. Anyone the file is shared with can open it; others are told they don't have it
- Copy link. Every file and folder on the start screen has Copy link in its menu. Paste it into a chat or email, and opening it takes you straight there

## Improvements

- The dropdown list ticks the current choice and has Edit choices at the bottom
- Excel and LibreOffice files keep the colours of single-choice dropdowns, and PDFs show the pills

## Downloads

- Linux: the AppImage, or the .deb for Debian and Ubuntu
- Mac: the .dmg for Apple silicon (arm64) or Intel (x64)
- Windows: PearOffice-Setup. Windows may warn that the app is from an unknown publisher; choose More info, then Run anyway

The desktop app tells you when a new version is out.
