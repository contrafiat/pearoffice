PearOffice 1.1.0 opens and saves OpenDocument files, so you can work with LibreOffice and OpenOffice users.

## New Features

- **Spreadsheets in .ods.** Open and save LibreOffice spreadsheets with their formulas, charts, formatting, column widths, frozen rows and columns, colour rules and data validation. A filter saved in the file comes in too
- **Documents in .odt.** Open and save LibreOffice documents with their headings, lists, checklists, tables, pictures, links and text formatting
- **Slides in .odp.** Open and save LibreOffice Impress presentations with their text, shapes, lines and arrows, pictures, charts and speaker notes

## Downloads

- **Linux:** the AppImage, or the .deb for Debian and Ubuntu
- **Mac:** the .dmg for Apple silicon (arm64) or Intel (x64)
- **Windows:** PearOffice-Setup. Windows may warn that the app is from an unknown publisher; choose More info, then Run anyway
- **Seeder for Linux servers:** pearoffice-seeder for x64 or arm64

The desktop app tells you when a new version is out.
