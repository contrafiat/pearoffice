PearOffice is spreadsheets, documents and slides you share with a link and edit together, straight between your devices. No account, no subscription and no company holding a copy of your files.

SPREADSHEETS, DOCUMENTS AND SLIDES

- Spreadsheets with around 400 formulas, charts, filters, comments, cell formats and colour rules
- Documents with headings, lists, tables, pictures and links
- Slides with layouts, shapes, pictures, charts and themes, and a presenter view
- Open and save Excel, Word and PowerPoint files, plus CSV, Markdown and PDF

EDIT TOGETHER, LIVE

Send someone an invite link. Each link works once and lasts 24 hours, and you choose whether they can edit or only view. Everyone in a file sees each other's changes as they happen, and who is working where. The owner can remove someone at any time, and they see nothing new after that.

NO ACCOUNT, NO SERVER

Your files live on your devices and the devices of the people you share them with, and sync directly between them, encrypted. There is nothing to sign up for. Your identity is a key made on your device, and a 12-word recovery phrase brings it back on a new device.

USE ALL YOUR DEVICES

Link your phone, tablet and computers once and they keep the same files. PearOffice also runs on Mac, Windows, Linux, iPhone and iPad.

AN OPTIONAL SEEDER

Files sync while the people in them are online. A seeder is a device you leave running, such as a home server, an Umbrel or StartOS box or your own computer, that keeps an encrypted copy so everyone catches up even when nobody else is online. It cannot read your files.

WHEN A DIRECT CONNECTION IS NOT POSSIBLE

Some networks block direct connections between devices. PearOffice can then fall back to a relay run by PeerLoom, which passes on encrypted data it cannot read and keeps nothing. You can turn this off in Settings.

PearOffice has no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and is MIT licensed.
