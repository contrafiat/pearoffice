# Who has a sheet open, and where they are

**Status:** approved by Tim 2026-10-05 (share the cell; a setting to stay
hidden, on by default; colours from identity).

**Goal:** members see who else has a sheet open right now, which cell each
person is on and whether they are typing in it.

**Tier:** T2. A new Protomux channel between devices. Nothing stored
changes, and older builds keep working (they never open the channel).

## What people see

- In the sheet's header, the avatars of members who have it open get a
  green ring. Others stay as they are now.
- Each person's current cell gets an outline in their colour, with their
  name in a small tag above it. While they are typing in it, the tag shows
  "typing".
- Only in the same tab: someone on another tab shows on the avatar
  ("on Budget") and not in the grid.

"Open" means the sheet is on screen. A phone sent to the background, a
closed window or a sheet switched away from counts as closed.

## Scope

**Changes:**

- A new channel `pearsheet/presence/1` (`src/worklet/presence.js`), one per
  sheet per connection, with the sheet id as the channel id, opened the same
  way as `pearsheet/join/1`.
- New IPC: `sheet:presence` (UI to worklet: `{ sheetId, open, tab, row, col,
  editing }`) and the event `sheet:presence` (worklet to UI: the list of
  people present).
- UI: the ring on avatars, the outlines and name tags in the grid.

**Does not change:** anything in the Autobase or Hyperbee, the join
handshake, the seeder, invites or roles.

## Messages

All JSON over Hyperswarm's encrypted connection.

    hello  { t, sheetId, identity, device, at, sig }
    state  { t, open, tab, row, col, editing }
    bye    { t }

`hello` proves who is on the other end: `sig` is the sender's identity key
signing `{ purpose: 'pearsheet/presence/v1', sheetId, identity, device, at }`,
where `device` is the sender's swarm key. The receiver checks that

1. `device` is the key of this connection (`conn.remotePublicKey`),
2. the signature is valid and `at` is within 10 minutes of now,
3. `identity` is a current member of the sheet in its own copy.

Nothing else is sent or accepted on the channel until both sides pass.
The seeder is not a member, so it never passes. A removed person fails
check 3 as soon as the removal reaches the other device. Viewers are
members and can see others and be seen.

`state` is sent when it changes, at most 4 times a second, and every 20
seconds while open. A person not heard from for 60 seconds, or whose
connection closes, drops off. Presence lives only in memory.

## Compat

Older builds never open `pearsheet/presence/1`, so new builds see no
presence for them and they see none for anyone. Nothing breaks either way.

## Verify

- `test/presence.test.js` over a local DHT testnet: two members see each
  other's state and typing; a seeder and a stranger on the same topic get
  nothing; a removed member gets nothing; a person drops off when the
  connection closes and after 60 seconds of silence; a forged `hello` (wrong
  device, wrong signature, old `at`) is refused.
- Desktop smoke: a presence event from the worklet draws the outline and
  the avatar ring.
- An emulator and the TCL (suite rule 15): each sees the other's cell move
  and "typing".

## Rollback

Remove the channel and the UI. Nothing was stored, so there is nothing
to clean up.

## Decided

1. **The cell is shared**, with "typing".
2. **A setting to stay hidden:** "Show others when I have a sheet open",
   on by default. Off sends nothing at all, not even `hello`, so with
   it off you do not see others either (a channel needs both hellos).
3. **Colours** come from each person's identity (as the avatar placeholder
   does), so they match on every device.
